# Definition of Ready

A backlog item is ready for Sprint commitment only when every applicable condition is satisfied.

- [ ] Purpose, user or engineering value, and expected outcome are understood.
- [ ] Scope and out-of-scope boundaries are explicit and small enough for one Sprint.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are objective, testable, and describe observable outcomes.
- [ ] Governing requirements, ADRs, DES-001 sections, and PLAN-001 WP section are identified.
- [ ] Requirement identifiers have been checked against REG-002; none are invented.
- [ ] Predecessors, dependencies, external needs, and sequencing constraints are identified.
- [ ] Architecture, data, security, privacy, and secret-handling impacts are considered.
- [ ] Expected unit, application, integration, API, security, configuration, frontend, or E2E tests are identified as applicable.
- [ ] Expected files or owning boundaries are stated without authorizing broad changes.
- [ ] Installation, runtime-command, database, migration, and documentation impacts are known.
- [ ] Major unknowns are resolved or represented by a justified, time-boxed SPIKE.
- [ ] Estimate uses 1, 2, 3, 5, or 8 points; anything larger is split.
- [ ] No unresolved requirement, Accepted ADR, architecture, or DES contradiction exists.
- [ ] The ticket includes the blast-radius stop rule.
- [ ] Product Owner and delivery team can make an informed commitment decision.

Readiness does not guarantee Sprint selection. Capacity and dependencies are decided at Sprint Planning; no velocity is inferred before empirical history exists.
