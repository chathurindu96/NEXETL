## Refinement policy

- WP-02/WP-03 detailed tickets remain candidates until Sprint Planning.
- WP-04+ tickets are generated progressively when predecessors and design inputs are ready.
- Bugs are created from observed defects. Technical-debt items require concrete evidence and an explicit remediation outcome.
- A future item is not assigned to a Sprint merely because a dependency order is known.
